# **App Name**: NyaySathi

## Core Features:

- AI Legal Chat tool: A conversational AI tool that uses reasoning to provide instant legal guidance on Indian laws (IPC, Family, Tax) in simple terms.
- Multilingual Query Support: AI-powered input and output capabilities for English, Marathi, and Hindi, ensuring accessibility for all Indian citizens.
- Legal Voice Guidance: Voiceover system providing clear text-to-speech output of legal advice for hands-free or accessibility needs.
- Judgment & Precedent Retrieval tool: An AI research tool that identifies relevant High Court and Supreme Court case laws based on user query context.
- Proximity Lawyer Finder: Interactive directory of legal professionals mapped by location to facilitate direct consultation for specific cases.
- Intelligent Document Scanner: AI-driven OCR tool to extract key legal entities and summaries from uploaded PDFs or physical legal document photos.
- Responsive Legal Dashboard: A premium central hub for tracking queries, reading judgment references, and accessing the 24/7 AI portal.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary action color: #ac4c27 (Rust); Main background: #050404 (Black/Dark).
- Accents: #38110d (Dark Accent) and #a4745c (Warm Highlight); Borders and structural depth: #230806 (Mid Dark).
- Primary font: 'Poppins' for a contemporary, precise, and authoritative legal feel.
- Fine-lined gold-toned icons representing scales of justice, gavels, and transcript waves.
- Immersive landing page featuring a hero input box that expands from a slim bar into a full conversational suite.
- Sophisticated scroll-triggered reveals, 60fps parallax hero effects, and interactive ripple effects on CTA buttons.
